Part 3 — Lesson 1.9 · What's the pattern?

Split, apply, combine — a pivot table is one line.

Clean rows answer nothing on their own. You have to group them. Choose what goes down the side, what goes across the top, and how to squash the numbers in between — the grid and the pandas line update together, so you can see exactly what your choice did.
